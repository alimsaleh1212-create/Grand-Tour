# Hand-Scoring Rubric

This file defines the anchor points used to score the five subjective features consistently across all 160 destinations. Scoring relative to fixed anchors prevents score drift (where early rows use a different mental scale than later rows).

---

## activity_density (0–10)

Measures how many notable activities and attractions exist per geographic footprint — not just count, but density.

| Score | Anchor | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Tokyo | Infinite things to do within any 1 km radius |
| 9 | Barcelona | Dense city packed with must-see activities |
| 8 | Queenstown | Small town, but activity-per-km² ratio is extreme (bungy, skydive, ski, jet boat) |
| 7 | Budapest | Rich activity but slower pace than Barcelona |
| 6 | Lisbon | Good density, some travel time between sites |
| 5 | Reykjavik | Activities spread over wide areas (Golden Circle etc.) |
| 4 | Namibia | Vast empty country, activities far apart |
| 3 | Saharan outpost | A few notable things, hours of driving between |
| 1 | Remote wilderness | One thing to see/do, nothing nearby |

|---|---|---|
