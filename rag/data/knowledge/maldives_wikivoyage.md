# Maldives — Ultimate Island Relaxation
*Style: Relaxation | Region: Asia*

The Maldives is an archipelago of 1,200 coral islands scattered across 90,000 square kilometres of the Indian Ocean, southwest of Sri Lanka. Only 200 islands are inhabited; roughly 150 host tourist resorts. The country sits barely 1 metre above sea level on average — it is among the world's most climate-vulnerable nations. Its appeal is singular and undiluted: overwater bungalows, house reefs metres from shore, and turquoise water so clear that the sand is visible at 10-metre depths.

## The Resort Experience

The Maldives is almost exclusively a resort destination. Most visitors book an all-inclusive resort on a private island and rarely leave it. The standard offering includes:

- Overwater or beach villa accommodation
- House reef snorkelling (typically 10–15 metres from shore)
- PADI-certified dive centre with daily dive trips
- Sunset dolphin-watching excursions
- Island hopping and sandbank picnic trips
- Seaplane transfers (the iconic 30-minute flight over the atolls)

**Luxury benchmark:** A mid-range overwater villa at a five-star resort costs USD 400–700/night for two, all-inclusive. Private pool villas at flagship properties (Soneva Jani, Four Seasons Kuda Huraa, One&Only Reethi Rah) run USD 1,500–5,000/night.

## Budget Travel

The Maldives has a small but viable budget-travel circuit on local inhabited islands, particularly Maafushi, Thoddoo, and Guraidhoo. Budget guesthouses run USD 60–120/night. Bikini Beach (alcohol-free zones on local islands have designated swimwear beaches) is available. Local ferries connect islands for USD 3–10. This path costs USD 60–100/day and offers a fraction of the five-star experience but genuine Maldivian village life.

## Water Activities

**Snorkelling:** The house reefs at most resorts are among the world's best. Encounter turtles, reef sharks, manta rays, and Napoleon fish without a boat ride. The North Malé Atoll reefs are particularly dense.

**Diving:** The Maldives is among the world's top 5 dive destinations. Thilas (submerged reef pinnacles) attract dense fish aggregations. Hammerhead sharks gather at Rasdhoo Atoll; whale sharks frequent South Ari Atoll year-round.

**Surfing:** Breaks in the Malé South Atoll (Chickens, Cokes) are world-class — hollow left-hand barrels. May–October is the surfing season (southwest monsoon). Liveaboard surf charters explore outer atolls.

## Practical Information

**Getting there:** Male International Airport (Velana) receives direct flights from Dubai, Singapore, Colombo, and European hubs (Heathrow, Frankfurt, Istanbul, Doha). Most resort transfers are by speedboat (30–90 min) or seaplane (20–35 min).

**Cost per day:** USD 300–1,000+ at resorts (including accommodation). Budget island circuit: USD 60–100/day.

**Best time:** November–April (dry season, northeast monsoon). May–October brings rain and surf but prices drop 30–40% and whale shark sightings peak.

## What to Expect

The Maldives is not a destination for museums, cities, nightlife, culture, or adventure sports on land. It is unambiguously the world's premier **Relaxation** destination — a place engineered for passive enjoyment of extraordinary natural beauty. The experience is essentially the same across resorts; the variables are price and reef quality.
